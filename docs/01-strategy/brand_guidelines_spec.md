## 01 Executive Brand Architecture & Strategy

### 1. Brand Purpose
To empower small and medium business owners in Mexico to take full control of their cash flow, professionalize their client interactions, and eliminate administrative friction without needing complex, costly enterprise software.

### 2. Core Brand Principles
1. **Confianza Absoluta (Absolute Trust)**: Financial security, SAT CFDI 4.0 compliance, and Banxico SPEI validation built into every interaction.
2. **Simplicidad Radical (Radical Simplicity)**: 0-setup, mobile-native, 1-tap WhatsApp sharing, designed for zero-training onboarding.
3. **Agilidad Operativa (Operational Agility)**: Turn 20-minute Excel quote creation and manual payment chasing into 2-minute mobile tasks.
4. **Empatía Pyme (SMB Empathy)**: Speaking the authentic language of Mexican business owners (*Don Roberto* and *Lic. Mariana*) with respect, warmth, and clarity.

### 3. Voice, Tone & Language Rules

| Attribute | We ARE | We Are NOT | Example (Good Copy) |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Directness** | Clear, actionable, and benefit-driven | Corporate, bureaucratic, or academic | *"Visualiza exactamente quién te debe y cobra a tiempo."* |
| **Authenticity** | Culturally tuned to Mexican commerce | Generic translation or Silicon Valley buzzwords | *"Manda tu cotización en 2 min con tu logotipo y recíbela firmada por WhatsApp."* |
| **Tone** | Confident, encouraging, and authoritative | Pushy, patronizing, or informal to a fault | *"Sin contratos forzosos. Cancela en cualquier momento."* |

## 03 Visual Identity System

### 1. Color Palette Tokens & Hierarchy

```
Primary Surfaces (Dark Mode Dominance):
  • Deep Slate Base:    hsl(222, 47%, 4%)   / #090d16 / slate-950
  • Surface Card:       hsl(222, 47%, 8%)   / #0f172a / slate-900
  • Border Subtle:      hsl(217, 33%, 17%)  / #1e293b / slate-800

Brand Accent Tokens:
  • Emerald Gold/Green: hsl(160, 84%, 39%)  / #10b981 / emerald-500  (Cashflow, Growth, Primary CTAs)
  • Emerald Glow:       hsl(160, 84%, 48%)  / #34d399 / emerald-400  (Text accents & Icons)
  • Indigo Deep:        hsl(239, 84%, 67%)  / #6366f1 / indigo-500   (Tech leadership & System badges)

Functional Status Colors:
  • Success (Cobrado):  #10b981 (Emerald)
  • Warning (Vence Hoy):#f59e0b (Amber)
  • Overdue (Atrasado): #ef4444 (Rose/Red)
```

### 2. Typography System
- **Display & Headings**: `Outfit` / `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Extra Bold / Black, tight tracking `-0.02em`).
- **Body & Data UI**: `Inter` or modern system sans-serif (Medium / Regular, high legibility at 14px–16px).
- **Financial & Monetary Numbers**: `JetBrains Mono` / Tabular Numerals (`font-mono text-3xl font-black`).

### 3. Iconography & Mark Guidelines
- **Primary Icon Mark**: Rounded 2X-Large tile featuring an isometric building/growth node merged with a checkmark element (`Building2` + `Check`).
- **Surface Styling**: Glassmorphism with `backdrop-blur-xl`, `border border-slate-800`, and `shadow-2xl shadow-emerald-950/40`.
