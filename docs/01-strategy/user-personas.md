# User Personas: Business Helper

> **Research-Grounded Buyer & User Segment Profiles**
>
> Detailed, vivid profiles for **Business Helper** representing our core Mexican SMB user segments. Designed to build empathy and guide product decisions, UX flows, feature priorities, and messaging.

---

## Persona 1: Don Roberto — "El Dueño Tradicional"

### 01 Persona Name
* **Name**: Don Roberto — The Traditional SMB Owner
* **Photo Suggestion**: A friendly, sharp 45-year-old Mexican business owner in a polo shirt, sitting at a desk with an iPhone in hand and a printed invoice folder nearby.

### 02 Demographics
* **Age**: 45
* **Job Title & Seniority**: Founder & Owner
* **Company Size**: 8 employees (3 sales/warehouse, 2 delivery drivers, 2 admin, 1 external accountant)
* **Industry**: Construction Materials & Wholesale Supplies (Distribuidora del Norte)
* **Location**: Monterrey, Nuevo León, Mexico
* **Monthly Business Revenue**: $800,000 – $1,500,000 MXN
* **Technical Skill**: Non-technical (uses WhatsApp, Mobile Banking Apps, Facebook, Excel basics)

### 03 Goals
* **Product Goals**:
  * See **"Quién me debe y cuánto"** in under 5 seconds from his mobile phone.
  * Send professional, branded quotes to clients over WhatsApp while standing on the warehouse floor.
  * Get paid faster without personally calling clients to chase overdue SPEI transfers.
* **Role Goals**:
  * Keep the business profitable and cash-flow positive so he never misses biweekly payroll.
  * Avoid tax penalties from SAT due to mismatched invoices or improper RFC data.
  * Spend less time dealing with paperwork and more time building key client relationships.
* **Jobs to Be Done**:
  * *When a client asks for a price quote on WhatsApp*, **I want to** generate and send a branded, formatted quote link in 2 minutes, **so I can** close the deal before the competitor does.
  * *When invoices/milestones become overdue*, **I want** automated WhatsApp payment reminders sent to the client, **so I can** collect payments without feeling awkward or aggressive.

### 04 Pain Points
* **Current Frustrations**:
  1. **Uncertain Cash Flow & Hidden Debts**:
     * *Pain*: Doesn't know exact total receivables until his accountant sends a monthly PDF summary.
     * *Frequency*: Weekly.
     * *Severity*: Blocking (causes payroll anxiety).
     * *Current Workaround*: Asks his assistant to compile a manual Excel sheet every Friday afternoon.
  2. **PDF Quotes Sent via WhatsApp Get Lost**:
     * *Pain*: Creates quotes in Word/Excel, exports to PDF, sends via WhatsApp. Clients ignore them or ask for revisions, causing version confusion.
     * *Frequency*: Daily (5–10 quotes/day).
     * *Severity*: Painful (lost sales opportunities).
     * *Current Workaround*: Resends PDFs repeatedly or makes verbal agreements over phone calls.
  3. **Chasing SPEI Proof Receipts**:
     * *Pain*: Clients claim "ya te transferí" but don't send the SPEI receipt or tracking key (*Clave de Rastreo*).
     * *Frequency*: 3–4 times per week.
     * *Severity*: Painful (wastes time matching bank statements).
     * *Current Workaround*: Manually checks Banxico or bank app while on the phone with the client.

### 05 User Journey
* **Discovery**: Learns about Business Helper through a recommendation in a local Cámara de Comercio (CANACO) WhatsApp group or a post shared by his accountant.
* **Evaluation**: Opens the website on his iPhone. Doesn't want a sales call — wants to see a 1-minute video showing the WhatsApp quote feature. Checks the $599 MXN/mo price and decides it's cheap enough to try without approval.
* **Adoption**: Signs up on mobile. Skips complex settings. Inputs his business name, RFC, uploads his logo from photos, and creates his first quote for a real client immediately.
* **Ongoing Usage**: Opens the app 4–5 times a day on his phone — mostly to check the **Centro de Control** dashboard, tap "Recordar por WhatsApp" for overdue payments, and send quick quotes.

### 06 Behavioral Traits
* **Technology Behavior**:
  * *Tool Adoption*: Pragmatist / Late Majority. Only adopts tools that solve an immediate financial problem.
  * *Learning Style*: Explores independently on mobile; hates reading documentation or long user guides.
  * *Platform Preference*: **Mobile-first** (85% iPhone / 15% Desktop).
* **Work Style**:
  * *Decision Making*: Intuition & Cash-driven ("If I have money in the bank, we're good").
  * *Communication*: WhatsApp voice notes and phone calls. Hates long emails.
  * *Pace*: Fast-moving, action-oriented, easily interrupted by walk-in customers or deliveries.
* **Personality**: Pragmatic, direct, protective of his business, skeptical of complex software ("No quiero un software de la NASA").

### 07 Quotes from User Research
> *"El sábado tengo que pagar nómina y el viernes a las 6 PM ando revisando Excel por Excel a ver quién no me ha pagado el anticipo. Es un infierno."*
> — Context: Interview regarding weekly operational headaches.

> *"Yo no voy a sentarme a usar una computadora todo el día. Mi negocio se hace en la bodega y en WhatsApp. Si el sistema no funciona desde mi celular, no me sirve."*
> — Context: Evaluating software preferences.

> *"Le mandas el PDF por WhatsApp al cliente y se le pierde en el chat. Luego te dice que nunca se lo mandaste. Necesito que vean la cotización clara y le den clic en Aceptar."*
> — Context: Discussion on sales proposal friction.

### 08 Product Implications
* **Design Implications**:
  * Ultra-clean, high-contrast mobile UI with large tap targets (buttons >= 48px).
  * Big monetary numbers on the dashboard ("$145,000 MXN Por Cobrar") in bold text.
  * One-tap WhatsApp share buttons integrated everywhere.
* **Feature Priority**:
  * **P0**: Mobile Quote Generator + One-tap WhatsApp link sharing.
  * **P0**: "Quién me debe" Accounts Receivable Summary dashboard.
  * **P0**: Client SPEI receipt upload page.
  * **P2**: Complex inventory tracking (He doesn't want to input 500 SKU codes upfront).
* **Messaging**:
  * **Value Prop**: *"Manda cotizaciones por WhatsApp y cobra a tiempo sin batallar."*
  * **Tone**: Direct, simple, practical, 100% Mexican business terms (cotización, cobranza, anticipo, SPEI).
  * **Competitors He Compares Us To**: Excel, WhatsApp, paper notebooks, legacy CONTPAQi.

## Persona 2: Licenciada Mariana — "La Administradora Eficiente"

### 01 Persona Name
* **Name**: Licenciada Mariana — The Tech-Forward Agency Manager
* **Photo Suggestion**: A professional, focused 34-year-old woman in a modern office space working on a MacBook Air with a coffee mug and color-coded client pipeline sticky notes.

### 02 Demographics
* **Age**: 34
* **Job Title & Seniority**: Operations Director & Partner
* **Company Size**: 6 team members (3 designers, 2 developers, 1 account executive)
* **Industry**: Digital Marketing & Web Development Agency (Pixel & Code MX)
* **Location**: Mexico City (CDMX)
* **Monthly Business Revenue**: $250,000 – $600,000 MXN
* **Technical Skill**: Technical-adjacent (proficient with Notion, Slack, Google Workspace, Stripe, Canva, Figma)

### 03 Goals
* **Product Goals**:
  * Manage 15+ client service contracts with multiple payment milestones simultaneously.
  * Automate milestone payment reminders so her account executive doesn't waste time on manual follow-ups.
  * Generate SAT-compliant Facturación 4.0 CFDIs and monthly XML/PDF bundles for her external accountant in one click.
* **Role Goals**:
  * Scale agency revenue from $300K to $600K MXN/mo without adding administrative headcount.
  * Maintain a professional image with corporate clients (Moral Persons / Corporates).
  * Protect the agency against scope creep and delayed client payments.
* **Jobs to Be Done**:
  * *When a client approves a proposal*, **I want to** convert it into a formal legal agreement with milestones and SHA-256 digital seals, **so I can** ensure we are protected before work starts.
  * *At the end of every month*, **I want to** export all confirmed payments with client RFCs and XMLs, **so I can** hand them to my accountant without 10 hours of manual reconciliation.

### 04 Pain Points
* **Current Frustrations**:
  1. **Manual Milestone & Payment Tracking**:
     * *Pain*: Tracking 15 active projects with 3–4 milestones each across Google Sheets, Notion, and bank accounts leads to missed payment requests.
     * *Frequency*: Daily.
     * *Severity*: Painful (delayed agency cash flow).
     * *Current Workaround*: Maintains a complex color-coded Google Sheet that requires 2 hours of manual updating every day.
  2. **Scope Creep & Dispute Conversations**:
     * *Pain*: Clients ask for extra revisions or features not included in the original proposal because requirements weren't clearly locked in an accepted contract.
     * *Frequency*: 2–3 times per month.
     * *Severity*: Blocking (causes project delays and lost profit margins).
     * *Current Workaround*: Emails back and forth quoting old proposal threads.
  3. **Accountant Handoff Friction**:
     * *Pain*: Her accountant asks for invoices, RFCs, CFDI usages, and SPEI vouchers at month-end, forcing her to dig through inbox attachments.
     * *Frequency*: Monthly (last 3 days of the month).
     * *Severity*: Painful (wastes an entire weekend).
     * *Current Workaround*: Zips random PDF folders and sends them via WeTransfer.

### 05 User Journey
* **Discovery**: Searches Google for *"software para cotizaciones y cobranza agencias mexico"* or sees a targeted LinkedIn post about agency cash flow management.
* **Evaluation**: Opens the site on her MacBook. Reviews the feature comparison table, checks the $999 MXN/mo Empresa tier, verifies SAT CFDI 4.0 integration capabilities, and signs up for the 14-day free trial.
* **Adoption**: Sets up the company profile, imports 12 clients via CSV/Form, configures RFC and Facturapi credentials, and creates standard proposal templates for web development and retainer packages.
* **Ongoing Usage**: Uses the desktop dashboard during office hours to review the **Pipeline de Cotizaciones** and **Cobranza**, and uses the mobile web app on her phone while traveling to client meetings.

### 06 Behavioral Traits
* **Technology Behavior**:
  * *Tool Adoption*: Early Adopter / Pragmatist. Enthusiastic about SaaS tools that save operational time.
  * *Learning Style*: Explores independently, sets up templates, reads integration documentation when needed.
  * *Platform Preference*: **Desktop-first** in office (65% MacBook / 35% Mobile).
* **Work Style**:
  * *Decision Making*: Data-driven & Process-oriented. Values organized workflows and clean metrics.
  * *Communication*: Slack, Email, WhatsApp Business API.
  * *Pace*: Structured, deadline-driven, balances multiple ongoing client timelines.
* **Personality**: Organised, detail-oriented, ambitious, quality-focused, values professional aesthetics.

### 07 Quotes from User Research
> *"Mis diseñadores están trabajando en cambios que el cliente no ha pagado porque no tengo una forma rápida de ver si ya cayó la transferencia del hito 2."*
> — Context: Discussion on agency delivery vs. payment alignment.

> *"A fin de mes pierdo dos días enteros organizando las facturas XML, los comprobantes SPEI y los RFCs para el contador. Debería poder descargar todo en un ZIP de un clic."*
> — Context: Pain points around accounting handoffs.

> *"Si la cotización se ve bonita y profesional, el cliente no le pone peros al precio. La presentación importa muchísimo en B2B."*
> — Context: Discussion on proposal branding and aesthetics.

### 08 Product Implications
* **Design Implications**:
  * Sleek, modern desktop dashboard with tabbed views (Kanban Pipeline, Table List View, Financial Analytics).
  * Clean PDF and web rendering for client quotes with high-end typography and agency logo branding.
  * One-click CSV/ZIP export buttons for accounting handoffs.
* **Feature Priority**:
  * **P0**: Multi-item Quote Creator with custom line-item templates.
  * **P0**: Milestone Payment Timeline with status tags (`pending`, `requested`, `marked_paid`, `confirmed`).
  * **P1**: Facturapi SAT CFDI 4.0 one-click invoicing integration.
  * **P1**: Client Health Score metrics and payment delay analytics.
  * **P2**: Multi-user role permissions (Owner vs. Manager vs. Accountant).
* **Messaging**:
  * **Value Prop**: *"Centraliza tus cotizaciones, contratos y cobranza. Escala tu agencia sin caos administrativo."*
  * **Tone**: Professional, modern, empowering, efficiency-focused.
  * **Competitors She Compares Us To**: Notion + Google Sheets + Facturapi + Odoo.
